# CLAUDE.md

Starship Flight 15 (NET 13 Nov 2026, window 22:45–00:00 UTC, Pad 2): the first ship catch at Starbase. Pre-launch model on
Flight 14's zone-fitted orbit and calibrated ship (`~/sda/starship-ITF-14`, imported, not copied), the official Flight 15
timeline, the Flight 15 navigational warnings and the Mexican entry NOTAM (`navwarning/`, NGA and ICAO text, parsed by `work/flt15_zones.py`), a Super Heavy return
and a 3D page built from `~/launch/launch-page-template` with the `launch-page` skill.
Repo: github.com/exoplanet5/Starship-FLT15; GitHub Pages serves `main` → `/docs`, so a push to `main` deploys https://exoplanet5.github.io/Starship-FLT15/ (publish with the skill's `publish_page.py`, only when asked). README.md has the results, assumptions and sources. Everything runs with `~/.venvs/astro313/bin/python`.

## Pipeline (order matters)

1. `work/flt15_orbit.py`: plane (from `ift14_fit`), Flight 15 timeline (`flight-timeline.txt`), shaped ascent, booster. Reads `work/phase.json` for the period scale if it exists.
2. `work/flt15_descents.py --solve`: nominal stage 1 (solves Δv, one bank and the period scale straight to the tower → writes `phase.json`), nominal stage 2 on that orbit (`solve_corridor`: Δv, two banks and the switch time along the Mexican corridor and the routed approach), then the four contingencies and the sampled tracks → `work/descents.json`. About 15 min. Without `--solve` it only fills in missing solutions and tracks; `--corridor` redoes only stage 2 (about 4 min) and leaves the orbit and the contingencies alone.
3. `work/flt15_tle.py` → `work/tle.json`, `flt15_tle.txt` (after step 2: it uses the period scale).
4. `work/flt15_map.py` → the three PNGs (`global_map()`, `global_map(center=-150)` for the Pacific view, `approach_map()`). `work/flt15_zones.py` parses the warnings (`ZD` zone dicts with shapely polygons, `ZONES` for the page, `window_met` for window checks).
5. `work/flt15_export.py` → `export/*.json` and `export/registry.json`; then `new_page.py` (first mission without `--add`, the rest with `--add`); the menu order in `docs/data/profiles.json` was set by hand to nominal, Indian, early Hawaii, Chile, late Hawaii.

## Key facts and decisions

- **User inputs (8 Nov 2026):** same altitude and inclination as Flight 14; first full catch (booster and ship); more than 22 h to align with Starbase; contingencies as Flight 14 plus a late-mission Hawaii option; no Flight 15 warnings yet, adopt Flight 14's zones and re-time them; extend the model to reentry and catch. **10 Nov:** the six Flight 15 warnings (user cached them in `navwarning/`) replaced the stand-ins; the user expected "only minor adjustment"; the late-Hawaii pass change below is the one non-minor consequence and was flagged.
- **Flight 15 warnings (issued 7–9 Nov):** NAVAREA IV 1080/26 A, B launch 2245–0040Z; NAVAREA IV 1081/26 ship catch (Laguna Madre, Padre Island, Gulf off Starbase; north tip 26.77 N 97.47 W, Gulf apex 26.17 N 96.37 W) 2108–0014Z 14–15 Nov; HYDROPAC 3244/26 Indian Ocean 2253–0114Z (Flight 14 polygon, longer west end); HYDROPAC 3257/26 Midway = the Flight 14 North Pacific polygon 0130–0501Z; HYDROPAC 3254/26 Chile 0812–1137Z (wider than Flight 14's); HYDROPAC 3256/26 Micronesia → NE of Hawaii 2217–0144Z. Windows dated 14–20 Nov belong to 13–19 Nov launches. **Convention found:** each contingency window opens within a minute of the modeled descent entering its area at ~80 km (22:45Z launch) and closes one orbit + ~20 min after the first-pass splash, so each covers two passes; the model shows the first.
- **Official timeline** from spacex.com/launches/starship-flight-15 (fetched 8 Nov via Exa; the page is client-rendered, plain fetches return an empty shell). Window 22:45–00:00Z = 16:45–18:00 CST. `t0_max` on the page is 23:59:59 because the template needs a same-date interval.
- **Period scale +0.00134** (nodal period 89.63 min, as a 269 km mean orbit) is the one free orbit parameter, solved with the nominal Δv and bank for the official catch time. Without it the catch came 1 min 46 s late while the altitude at the official entry time (80 km) already matched Flight 14's convention. Flight 14's deorbit telemetry read 270 km, so a lower mean orbit is the physical reading. Do not retune it by hand; re-solve the nominal.
- **Return pass:** the 15th pass after insertion crosses Starbase's longitude ~400 km north of the pad; the ship banks south. The pass crosses the Kauai Channel at ~80 km (Honolulu 52 km, Lihue 79 km).
- **Mexican entry corridor, NOTAM B4288/26 (user cached it 12 Nov; "verify it aligns with the current profile, then update the page").** MMFR, SFC–UNL, areas C2 + C3, 2056–0014Z daily 14–20 Nov: 120.6 W 30.5 N off Baja California to the Rio Grande at 99.9 W 27.9 N (64 km NW of Laredo), 20–80 km wide, cut by the FIR boundary (west) and the border (east); no C1 and no Texas notice are cached. **Found:** time and direction fit (window opens at the official entry time; closes with the catch area), but the single 20° bank ran parallel 50–70 km south of the centreline, outside the corridor all the way. **Decision:** keep the orbit (period scale from stage 1, so contingencies and TLE are untouched) and re-solve the nominal in `solve_corridor`: Δv 69.35 m/s, bank 11.3° until T+22:19:07, then 27.1°; residuals = catch time (0.05 s), routed distance (0.2 km), corridor-centreline offsets at 1° steps weighted by 0.3 × the local half-width. Result: in the corridor T+22:19:27 (72 km) → T+22:26:05 (53 km), ≤15 km from the centreline, ≥8 km from the edges; Chihuahua is now 78 km away, Hermosillo 95 km, Laredo 31 km. What did not work: with the simulation aimed straight at the tower and the orbit fixed, no two-bank solution fits (5 s late or 20–50 km south); it closes only when the simulation flies the true routed distance. A constant second bank leaves the corridor through its south side near 100.8 W, hence the border waypoint in the overlay. The centreline is an assumption.
- **Late Hawaii = orbit 16, the pass after the Starbase pass (from HYDROPAC 3256/26, 10 Nov).** The user had chosen the 14th orbit before any warning existed (8 Nov: "14th orbit before starbase, not one more orbit"); the published window (T+23:32–26:59 after a 22:45Z launch) and polygon (SW end 9 N 154 E; NE lobe holds Flight 14's splash point) fit only the pass after the catch pass, so the model moved and the change was reported. Fixed 69 m/s, burn time and bank solved for Flight 14's splashdown 25.50 N 155.43 W: burn T+22:57:47 (81.8 min after the nominal, 7.8 min before the same orbital point), bank 19.3° S, enters the area T+23:32:25 at 81 km (window opens T+23:32:00), splash T+23:53:51, 1.7 g, 60 km inside the area's southern edge. The old 14th-orbit descent (T+21:19:46 → 18.3 N 165.5 W) lies in no published area or window. Alternative reading rejected: the nominal-timed burn one orbit later with the entry interface exactly at the area's SW end would need a glide shortened to 6000 km, a new and harsher entry profile.
- **Chile is solved to Flight 14's planned landing point** (29.9 S 86.9 W), not its landing time: with the period scale, a time-solved descent landed 3° east, outside the zone.
- **Early Hawaii** is Flight 14's as-flown burn re-timed by the 42 s later insertion (T+02:12:42) and solved to Flight 14's reported splashdown.
- **Indian Ocean** as on Flight 14: coast from SECO with +50 t payload (ballistic coefficient 475), bank 81° with one reversal to the zone's centre-line middle; 5.2 g.
- **Final approach overlay** (`route`, `reshape`, `approach_overlay` in `flt15_descents.py`): east of `START_LON` (103.5 W, T+22:24:29, 770 km to go) the ground path is a chord-length Hermite through `W_BORDER` (27.942 N, 99.942 W, the middle of the corridor's east end, tangent 108°), `W_LAGUNA` (26.62 N, 97.35 W: over the Laguna Madre / Padre Island 57 km north of the SPI town, inside the NAVAREA IV 1081/26 strip whose tip is 26.77 N) and `W_GULF` (26.18 N, 96.85 W: 36 km NE of the tower) to the tower, tangents 140°, 200°, 235°. The simulation is solved to fly this routed length, so `overlay_stretch` is 1.0000 (it was ×1.21 over the last 350 km before 12 Nov) and heights, speeds, times and spacing are the simulation's; `near` is recomputed on the re-shaped path. Do not use the raw simulated path's last minutes for the page: it comes down 74 km east of the tower (`sim_end`). The nominal track's last sample is snapped to the official catch time in `flt15_export.uniform_track`, because the page's validator rejects a powered interval that ends after the track (the solve leaves a few ms).
- **Booster:** ballistic coefficient 3400 kg/m² and the landing-burn start height 4.5 km are assumptions; the landing-burn start point east of the tower is iterated so the booster arrives from the Gulf. Boostback 46 m/s², 1.66 km/s. Cartoon-level fidelity; MECO state from the shaped ascent (66 km, 1.23 km/s ground, 0.9 km/s climb at T+2:19).
- **Zones** are the published Flight 15 polygons and windows (`flt15_zones.py` reads the NGA text in `navwarning/` with `read_warning` and ICAO NOTAMs with `read_notam`, items B/C/D and `AREA-xx:` rings of DDMMN DDDMMW; the corridor areas are keys `mexC2`, `mexC3`, role `entry`, on the page with the nominal's overflight times; `ZD[key]` dicts carry shapely polygons with `wrap` for the two dateline areas). The Flight 14 KML stand-ins and the re-timed windows are gone (git history). `ift14_fit` still supplies the plane fit and the ship; its own zones are no longer used here.
- **Indian Ocean** target = the middle of the new area's centreline (21.37 S 84.93 E, 45 km west of the Flight 14 target): bank 82.2°, 5.7 g, splash T+56:16.
- **Catch area** NAVAREA IV 1081/26 is in the page as role `landing` (impact = the catch for the nominal, note "Nominal return" for contingencies) and drawn on the approach map and its inset. The nominal is inside it from T+22:28:09 (42 km).
- **Template changes made for this mission** (in `~/launch/launch-page-template`, tests pass): `meta.orbit_end_met` (flight ends with a descent), `meta.companion` (second vehicle on its own track, used for Super Heavy), `meta.layer_labels`, `meta.zone_noun`, and segment `kind: 'starship'` in `vehicle-art.js` with a sunlit glint in `app.js`; `meta.layer_defaults` and `meta.layer_order` (10 Nov, user: sunlit plume and cities checkboxes off by default and placed after Borders & regions; `profile.js` exports `LAYERS`, the validator checks the names). **Symbols redrawn 10 Nov** (user: "concise and precise"): `starship` is now an ogive nose (29 % of the length), tiled left side, aft flaps and small forward flaps on the nose, flat base with no skirt box; new `kind: 'superheavy'` (hot-stage ring, two grid fins edge-on with the V3 booster's third behind the barrel, engine section; no chines, unconfirmed for V3); `stack.width_boost` (1.4 here, default 2, 1 = true scale: true scale is an 8 px sliver at marker size); `companion.tail_first` (booster flies engines-first and holds its ignition attitude through each burn). The ship still points its nose along the velocity through the deorbit and landing burns. Documented in `PROFILE_FORMAT.md`. After a template change, copy `docs/js/app.js` and `vehicle-art.js` into this project's `docs/js` or rebuild with `new_page.py`.
- **Maps:** 13.2 in at 300 dpi (3960 px); `ift14_style` fonts scaled 0.6. Sun 0° yellow and Sun −12° cyan, both solid (`linestyles='solid'`, or matplotlib dashes negative levels). Orbit is steel blue so it does not read as the −12° line; it is drawn one more, fainter revolution to the late-Hawaii burn. The approach map's inset must have `set_zorder(20)` or the parent's city labels show through it; its extent is 97.95–96.40 W, 25.75–26.85 N to hold the lagoon approach and the catch area. The approach map draws the Mexican corridor; `Nuevo Laredo` is in `PLACES` for distances but skipped on the map (it hides Laredo).

## Page

- **Page upgrades (user, 8 Nov):** mission change keeps camera/frame/T0/MET (`selectMission` passes them in the URL); only two orbits drawn (`meta.track_window`, window centred on the current MET, slides with playback) and the Global view centred on the equator; ECEF/GCRS switch in the title bar (`state.frame`, `?frame=`): the Earth stays in scene coordinates, the path group turns by −Ω·MET with samples rotated by +Ω·t_i, and the camera anchor longitude drifts at −Ω so the viewer is inertially fixed; the legend wraps inside its box. The Indian mission has no orbit segment, so no window. Verified by a workflow: numeric sign check (orbit repeats to 0.45°/rev and the Sun to 0.2°/5 h in the rotated frame; the opposite sign gives 40° and 133°), adversarial code review (8 findings, all confirmed by two refuters and fixed: the drift is now an incremental `cam.rotate` about the pole so zoom, heading, the pole and a held drag are untouched and it pauses during gestures, flights and follow mode; label occlusion uses world positions under the rotated group; the switch is also a panel row and the title copy hides below 860 px; picking scans every windowed sample and breaks screen ties toward the current time; the windowed line keeps `dashed`), and headless browser tests (`frame_tests.py` in the session scratchpad, 9/9).

`docs/` is a build of the template: do not edit `docs/js` here; change the template, rebuild with `new_page.py`, then `check_page.py`. A data-only update (12 Nov, the corridor) is `flt15_export.py` and then `new_page.py --add` for the five missions in the menu order; that keeps `docs/js` as published, which matters because the template has since moved on (reentry missions, uncommitted work for another page) and has not been re-checked with this mission.

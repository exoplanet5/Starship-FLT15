# Hazard zones adopted from Flight 14

No navigational warnings for Flight 15 had been issued when this model was built (checked 8 Nov 2026).
The KML files here are the Flight 14 warnings (NAVAREA IV 922/26 launch areas A and B, HYDROPAC 2751/26 Indian Ocean,
NAVAREA XII 657/26 = HYDROPAC 2761/26 North Pacific, HYDROPAC 2750/26 South Pacific west of Chile), copied from
~/sda/starship-ITF-14/navwarning. SpaceX states that the Flight 15 contingency areas (early- and late-mission Hawaii,
mid-mission Chile) are the same precleared areas used on Flight 14. Their daily windows in the page and maps are
NOT published values: they are the Flight 14 windows re-timed to the Flight 15 launch window (22:45–00:00 UTC).
Replace these files and the windows in work/flt15_zones.py when the Flight 15 warnings appear.
